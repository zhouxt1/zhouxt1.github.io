About

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at Penn State, advised by Dr. Syed Rafiul Hussain and Dr. Gang Tan. I work at the intersection of computer security and programming languages: verified parsers, bug finding through fuzzing and differential testing, and undefined behavior in ‘safe’ languages like Rust.
